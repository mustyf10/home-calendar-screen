Every physical update is at least 180 seconds (three minutes) apart. A full redraw is due daily, even if the image is unchanged or the server is offline. This safety redraw takes priority over the night pause, but never over the minimum spacing; it may happen just before the pause starts. Offline redraws keep the connection warning until a successful recovery update.

These safeguards need the Pi running, a valid clock and a working display; they cannot guarantee timing while it is stopped or without power.
